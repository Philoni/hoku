# Code of Conduct

Hoku adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)**
as its code of conduct. It applies to all project spaces – issues, discussions, pull requests, reviews – and to
anyone representing the project elsewhere.

> **Deutsch:** Hoku folgt dem Contributor Covenant 2.1 ([Übersetzungen](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/translations/)).
> Kurz: respektvoll, freundlich und konstruktiv miteinander umgehen – egal wer du bist und wie viel Erfahrung du hast.

## In short
- Be welcoming, patient and kind. Assume good intent; this is a volunteer project.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Focus on what is best for the people using Hoku.
- Not acceptable: harassment, insults or personal attacks, sexualised language or imagery, trolling,
  publishing others' private information (for example addresses, bridge keys or home details) without permission,
  and any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting
If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately via
[GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/Philoni/hoku/security/advisories/new) (mention "Code of Conduct" in the title).
Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement
The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this
code of conduct, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban contributors, following the enforcement guidelines
(Correction, Warning, Temporary Ban, Permanent Ban) of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
